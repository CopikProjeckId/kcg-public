# Privacy Policy

_Korea Culture Game (“KCG”, “we”) — the KCG Android app (package `kcg.koreaculturegame`) and the web games it loads. Effective date: 2026-11-01._

## 1. What we collect

| Data | Why | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Google account identifier (subject ID), e-mail address, display name, profile picture | Creating and signing in to your KCG account in the app | Google Sign-In, with your consent |
| Telegram user ID, username, name, profile picture, language | Signing in to the web games when they are opened from Telegram | Telegram Mini App init data |
| Gameplay data: scores, progress, game currency (KCG) balance, items, best times, play history | Running the games, rewards, rankings, fraud prevention | Generated while you play |
| Purchase records (product, time, order ID) — once in-app purchases are offered | Delivering what you bought, refunds, fraud prevention | Google Play Billing |
| Technical data: IP address, device/OS/app version, request logs | Security, debugging, abuse prevention | Automatically when the app talks to our servers |

We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers. The app does not currently show third-party ads.

## 2. How we use it

- To operate your account and the games, keep your balance and progress, and show rankings.
- To prevent cheating and abuse (for example server-side validation of game results).
- To respond to support requests.

We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for advertising profiles.

## 3. Sharing

Data is processed by our own servers. We rely on these processors: Google (Sign-In, Play Billing), Telegram (sign-in when a web game is opened from Telegram) and our hosting providers. We disclose data only when required by law.

## 4. Retention and deletion

Account and gameplay data are kept while your account exists. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by e-mail (below); we delete it within 30 days unless we must keep purchase records for legal reasons.

## 5. Children

KCG is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from them.

## 6. Security

Traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Access to production data is restricted to the operating team.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on your country you may have rights to access, correct, delete or export your data, or to object to processing. Contact us and we will respond within 30 days.

## 9. Changes

We will post updates on this page and change the effective date above.
